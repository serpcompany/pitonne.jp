## Treatment Overview

Stem Cell Therapy at Pitonne is offered as a referral-based regenerative medicine support service for clients interested in advanced physician-led care through our trusted partner clinics.

For autologous stem cell therapy, treatment is performed in person at the partner clinic after a full medical evaluation. Pitonne does not directly perform the stem cell procedure itself. Instead, we provide consultation support, referral coordination, attendance during key counseling visits when needed, and personalized follow-up before and after treatment.

The treatment introduced through our partner clinic is based on autologous adipose-derived stem cell therapy, in which stem cells are collected from the patient's own fat tissue, processed in a controlled facility, and later administered according to the physician's treatment plan. Because the cells are derived from the patient's own tissue, this is positioned as a personalized regenerative medicine option for selected cases under physician judgment. The general treatment flow typically includes medical evaluation, blood testing, fat tissue collection, cell culture over several weeks, treatment administration, and follow-up care.

This option may be considered for clients exploring regenerative medicine approaches for concerns such as joint symptoms, chronic musculoskeletal discomfort, recovery support, hair related concerns, or age related wellness goals, depending on physician evaluation and clinical suitability. The partner clinic's materials describe autologous adipose-derived stem cells as a regenerative option being considered in areas such as joint conditions, chronic pain support, hair growth support, and age related care.

At Pitonne, our role is to make this process feel more accessible, private, and supported. We help clients understand the treatment pathway, prepare for the consultation, and stay supported throughout the experience.

## How It Works

### 1. Initial Consultation with Pitonne

We begin with a private consultation to understand your concerns, goals, symptoms, and treatment interests. We explain whether stem cell therapy may be worth discussing with a partner physician.

### 2. Referral to Partner Clinic

If appropriate, we introduce you to a trusted partner clinic for a formal medical evaluation. The physician determines whether treatment is suitable based on your symptoms, medical history, examination, and any required testing.

### 3. In-Clinic Evaluation and Preparation

If you are a candidate for treatment, the partner clinic will guide you through the clinical process. This may include medical examination, blood testing, fat tissue collection, and preparation for cell culture before treatment.

### 4. Stem Cell Treatment at the Partner Clinic

The actual stem cell procedure is performed at the partner clinic under physician supervision. Depending on the treatment plan, administration may involve IV infusion or local injection, as determined by the physician.

### 5. Pitonne Follow-Up and Ongoing Support

Pitonne remains available for follow-up support, communication assistance, and coordination before and after treatment. When needed, we can also provide attendance support during important consultation or counseling visits.

## Helpful Notes

- Stem cell therapy is not performed directly by Pitonne and is provided only through trusted partner clinics.
- All treatment decisions are made by the partner physician after medical evaluation.
- Suitability depends on your symptoms, goals, medical history, and test results.
- The treatment flow may include blood testing, fat tissue collection, cell processing, treatment administration, and follow-up.
- Because this is advanced regenerative medicine, the treatment schedule may require multiple steps over several weeks.
- Pitonne's role is to provide guidance, coordination, and follow-up support throughout the process.
- Specific treatment methods, timing, and recommendations vary depending on the physician's clinical judgment.
