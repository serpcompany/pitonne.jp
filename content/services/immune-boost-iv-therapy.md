## Treatment Overview

Immune Boost IV Therapy is designed to deliver hydration, vitamins, antioxidants, and essential nutrients directly into the bloodstream for efficient absorption and condition support. This treatment is often chosen by clients who want additional wellness support during seasonal changes, busy periods, travel, or times of increased physical or mental stress.

It may help support immune function, recovery from fatigue, and overall resilience, making it a practical option for busy professionals, travelers, and anyone looking to maintain daily wellness and condition balance.

At Pitonne, selected treatments may be provided after an online medical consultation with a partner physician, followed by a personalized IV appointment at home, in a hotel, at the office, or at our partner clinic in Nishi Azabu.

## Key Benefits

- Supports immune function and overall wellness
- May help support the body during seasonal changes
- Supports recovery from fatigue and physical stress
- Helps maintain hydration and nutrient balance
- Promotes daily condition support and resilience

## How It Works

### 1. Consultation

We review your symptoms, goals, and health history to determine whether this treatment is appropriate for you.

### 2. Personalized Plan

Your treatment plan is tailored based on your condition, schedule, and wellness priorities. Timing, expectations, and any questions are reviewed before treatment begins.

### 3. Treatment and Follow-Up

Treatment is provided with comfort, safety, and practical wellness support in mind. Ongoing guidance may be provided depending on your treatment plan and goals.

## Helpful Notes

- Please follow any guidance provided before and after treatment.
- Staying hydrated and allowing time for recovery may help support your results.
- Treatment frequency may vary depending on your condition and wellness goals.
- If you have any questions after treatment, please contact us.
- This treatment is intended as wellness support and is not a substitute for regular medical care or treatment.

## Physician-Guided Wellness Care

This service supports wellness goals, but significant symptoms or illness should be discussed directly with a physician.
