## Treatment Overview

Immune boost IV therapy provides high-quality vitamins, antioxidants, and hydration designed to support immune system health. This treatment may help the body defend against illness, improve recovery from fatigue, and strengthen overall wellness. Many patients use immune IV therapy during seasonal changes or times of increased stress to help maintain optimal health.

- Helps the body during seasonal changes
