## Treatment Overview

AGA Medication at Pitonne is designed for men who want physician guided support for androgenetic alopecia (male pattern hair loss) in a private and convenient setting. Our standard AGA treatment set combines **oral minoxidil 5 mg** and **dutasteride (Zagallo)** as part of a personalized treatment plan when medically appropriate.

This service is often chosen by clients who want to address thinning hair, hair shedding, or long term hair retention without the need for a crowded clinic visit. Because hair loss treatment typically requires ongoing use and several months before visible changes may be noticed, continued treatment and regular follow up are important to help support long term results.

At Pitonne, AGA medication is provided through a **discreet online medical consultation** with a partner physician. This allows clients to discuss treatment privately while receiving physician guided recommendations based on their symptoms, medical history, and current medications.

If prescribed, medication may be arranged for **clinic pickup or local delivery** depending on your location and selected service option. Please note that **delivery fees apply separately unless you choose clinic pickup**.

## How It Works

### 1. Online Consultation

We review your hair concerns, treatment goals, medical history, and current medications through a private online consultation with a partner physician.

### 2. Personalized Treatment Plan

If appropriate, the physician will recommend the most suitable treatment approach based on your condition, risk factors, and long term goals. Ongoing use, expected timing, and follow up recommendations are reviewed before treatment begins.

### 3. Prescription and Follow Up

If prescribed, your medication can be arranged for clinic pickup or local delivery. Follow up consultations may be available depending on your treatment plan, and re examination fees may be waived for eligible follow up visits.

## Helpful Notes

- AGA medication is available only after physician assessment and prescription approval.
- Continued use is important, as hair loss treatment often requires several months before visible changes may be noticed.
- Ongoing treatment is usually needed to help maintain results over time.
- This treatment may not be appropriate for everyone, depending on your medical history and current medications.
- Please follow your provider's instructions carefully regarding dosage, timing, and follow up.
- Clinic pickup or local delivery may be available depending on your location and service option.
- Delivery fees apply separately unless you choose clinic pickup.
- This service is intended for physician guided prescription care and is not a substitute for in person medical evaluation when urgent symptoms are present.
