## Treatment Overview

### High Dose Vitamin C IV Therapy

Pitonne's High Dose Vitamin C IV Therapy delivers concentrated vitamin C through intravenous infusion as part of a physician-guided wellness plan.

This treatment is commonly chosen by clients who wish to incorporate high-dose vitamin C into their wellness routine, whether for general health management, beauty and skin wellness, or overall condition support.

Two dosage options are available: **25 g** and **50 g**. Your physician will recommend the most appropriate option based on your health condition and treatment goals.

For safety, clients receiving the **50 g** treatment are required to complete a **G6PD deficiency screening** before treatment.

## How It Works

### 1. Online Consultation

Your physician reviews your medical history, current health condition, and treatment goals.

### 2. Personalized Treatment Plan

Based on your consultation, the appropriate vitamin C dosage is recommended.

If you choose the 50 g treatment, a G6PD deficiency screening must be completed before your appointment.

### 3. Treatment & Follow-Up

A registered nurse administers your IV while monitoring your comfort throughout the treatment.

Follow-up support is available whenever needed.

---

## Helpful Notes

* Treatment is available only after physician assessment.
* A **G6PD deficiency screening** is required before receiving the 50 g treatment.
* Some clients may experience discomfort or irritation along the vein during the infusion. If this occurs, the infusion rate can usually be adjusted to improve comfort.
* Treatment may not be appropriate for everyone depending on their medical history and health condition.
* Please contact us if you have any questions before or after your appointment.

## Physician-Guided Wellness Care

High Dose Vitamin C IV Therapy is provided following an online consultation with a partner physician, who will recommend the most appropriate treatment based on your health condition and wellness goals.
