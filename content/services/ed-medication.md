## Treatment Overview

ED Medication at Pitonne is designed for men who want safe, discreet, and physician-guided support for erectile function. Prescription options such as Viagra or Cialis may help improve blood flow and support the ability to achieve and maintain an erection when medically appropriate.

This service is often chosen by clients who want a private and convenient option without the need for a crowded clinic visit. Depending on physician assessment, treatment may help support confidence, intimacy, and overall sexual wellness as part of a personalized care plan.

At Pitonne, ED medication is provided through an online medical consultation with a partner physician. If prescribed, medication may be arranged for clinic pickup or local delivery depending on your location and selected service option.

## Key Benefits

- Supports erectile function when medically appropriate
- Prescription options such as Viagra or Cialis may help improve blood flow
- May help support the ability to achieve and maintain an erection
- Discreet, physician-guided consultation and treatment planning
- Flexible options including clinic pickup or local delivery

## How It Works

### 1. Online Consultation

We review your symptoms, goals, medical history, and current medications through a private online consultation with a partner physician.

### 2. Personalized Treatment Plan

If appropriate, the physician will recommend the most suitable medication and dosage based on your needs, health background, and treatment goals.

### 3. Prescription and Follow-Up

If prescribed, your medication can be arranged for clinic pickup or local delivery. Follow-up consultations may be available depending on your treatment plan, and re-examination fees may be waived for eligible follow-up visits.

## Helpful Notes

- ED medication is available only after physician assessment and prescription approval.
- This treatment may not be appropriate for everyone, depending on your health history and current medications.
- Please follow your provider's instructions carefully regarding timing, dosage, and use.
- Medication options, onset time, and duration vary depending on the prescription selected.
- Clinic pickup or local delivery may be available depending on your location and service option.
- This service is intended for physician-guided prescription care and is not a substitute for emergency medical evaluation.

## Physician-Guided Wellness Care

Your consultation is used to recommend a safe, discreet, and personalized plan when ED medication is medically appropriate.
