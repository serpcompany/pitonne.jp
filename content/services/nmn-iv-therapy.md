A physician-guided IV therapy containing **300 mg of NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)**.

Treatment is provided following an online consultation with a partner physician and is tailored to your health condition and wellness goals.

---

## Treatment Overview

### NMN IV Therapy

Pitonne's NMN IV Therapy contains **300 mg of Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (NMN)** and is provided as private medical care under physician guidance.

NMN has gained increasing attention in the fields of wellness and healthy aging. This treatment is often chosen by clients who wish to incorporate NMN into their personal health and wellness routine.

Before treatment, a partner physician will review your medical history, current health condition, and treatment goals through an online consultation to determine whether NMN IV Therapy is appropriate for you.

---

## Key Benefits

* Contains **300 mg of NMN**
* Physician-guided online consultation
* Personalized treatment recommendations
* Available at your home, hotel, office, or our partner clinic
* Designed to support your individual wellness goals

---

## How It Works

### 1. Online Consultation

Your physician reviews your medical history, current health condition, and treatment goals.

### 2. Personalized Treatment Plan

If appropriate, NMN IV Therapy will be recommended based on your individual needs.

### 3. Treatment & Follow-Up

A registered nurse administers your IV while monitoring your comfort throughout the treatment.

Follow-up support is available whenever needed.

---

## Helpful Notes

* Treatment is available only after physician assessment.
* Each treatment contains **300 mg of NMN**.
* Treatment may not be appropriate for everyone depending on their medical history and health condition.
* NMN IV Therapy may be combined with certain other IV therapies when medically appropriate.
* Please contact us if you have any questions before or after your appointment.

---

## Physician-Guided Wellness Care

NMN IV Therapy is provided following an online consultation with a partner physician, who will recommend the most appropriate treatment based on your health condition and wellness goals.
