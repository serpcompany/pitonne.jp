Pitonne provides consultation support and referral coordination for autologous stem cell therapy through trusted partner clinics in Tokyo.

All treatments are performed by physicians at our partner clinics. Our team supports you throughout the process, from your initial consultation and appointment coordination to treatment-day assistance and follow-up care.

---

## Treatment Overview

### Stem Cell Therapy

Pitonne helps connect clients with trusted partner clinics offering autologous stem cell therapy in Tokyo.

We provide guidance before treatment, coordinate appointments, assist with the consultation process when needed, and offer ongoing support after treatment.

All medical consultations, treatment decisions, and procedures are performed by licensed physicians at our partner clinics.

If you are considering stem cell therapy and would like to learn more, our team is happy to answer your questions and help you get started.

---

## Key Benefits

* Referral to trusted partner clinics in Tokyo
* Support for autologous stem cell therapy
* Appointment coordination and consultation assistance
* Treatment-day support when needed
* Personalized follow-up after treatment

---

## How It Works

### 1. Initial Consultation

Tell us about your health concerns and treatment goals.

### 2. Referral to a Partner Clinic

We will help arrange an appointment with one of our trusted partner clinics.

### 3. Physician Consultation & Treatment

A physician at the partner clinic will evaluate your condition and determine whether stem cell therapy is appropriate for you.

If suitable, treatment will be performed at the partner clinic.

### 4. Follow-Up Support

Our team remains available to answer questions and provide ongoing support after your treatment.

---

## Helpful Notes

* All treatments are performed at our partner clinics.
* Eligibility for treatment is determined by the treating physician.
* Treatment details and fees will be explained during your consultation.
* Please feel free to contact us if you have any questions before or after your appointment.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Does Pitonne perform stem cell therapy?

No. Stem cell therapy is performed by licensed physicians at our trusted partner clinics. Pitonne provides consultation support, referral coordination, and follow-up assistance.

#### Can I speak with someone before making an appointment?

Yes. We are happy to answer your questions and help you determine whether a consultation at one of our partner clinics may be appropriate.

#### Is everyone eligible for stem cell therapy?

No. Eligibility is determined by the physician at the partner clinic after reviewing your medical history, current condition, and treatment goals.
