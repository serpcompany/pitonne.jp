# How to Get an Exosome IV in Tokyo: Mobile IV Flow and In-Clinic Booking at Pitonne

When considering an Exosome IV in Tokyo, many patients want to know whether they need to visit a clinic, whether treatment can be arranged at a hotel or residence, and what information is required before booking. For international travelers, foreign residents in Japan, and busy professionals, the treatment itself is only one part of the decision. Just as important is understanding how the experience is arranged.

At Pitonne | Stem Cell & IV Therapy, we offer both in-clinic appointments and mobile IV service within our coverage area. Whether you prefer to receive care at home, at your hotel, or in a private office setting, we aim to provide a discreet and medically guided experience tailored to your schedule and condition.

In this article, we explain how Pitonne's mobile IV service works, how it differs from an in-clinic visit, and what to prepare before making a reservation.

## Pitonne Offers Both In-Clinic and Mobile IV Appointments

At Pitonne, patients may choose between visiting the clinic or arranging a mobile IV appointment within our service area. This flexibility is especially helpful for travelers staying in Tokyo, patients who prefer private care in their hotel or residence, and those with demanding schedules who want to reduce travel time.

For patients who prefer to visit the clinic, **IV menu pricing is available with a JPY 5,000 discount**. The choice between mobile and in-clinic care depends on your preferences, location, schedule, and how you would like to receive treatment.

## How Mobile IV Therapy Works

### 1\. Inquiry and Booking

Please contact us with your preferred treatment, date, time, and service location. At Pitonne, mobile visit fees are included for locations within **5 km of Nishi Azabu**.

For locations beyond 5 km, an additional travel fee applies depending on distance.

* 5-10 km: JPY 11,000  
* 10-20 km: JPY 16,500  
* Over 20 km: from JPY 26,500

These are tax-included guide prices. Final details are confirmed at the time of booking.

### 2\. Submit Your Information

To prepare your visit safely and smoothly, we ask you to provide the following information in advance:

* Full name  
* Date of birth  
* Email address  
* Visit address  
* Medical history  
* Current medications  
* Allergy information

Depending on the treatment and your condition, we may ask for additional details as needed.

### 3\. Nurse Visit and Required Medical Review

A registered nurse will visit your home, hotel, office, or other requested location. Depending on your condition and the selected menu, an online medical consultation with our partner physician may also be conducted before treatment.

At Pitonne, convenience is important, but safety always comes first. For that reason, treatment is guided based on your condition on the day and any medical review that may be needed.

### 4\. Payment

Payment is generally accepted by **cashless payment such as credit card**. If you prefer to pay in cash, please contact us in advance.

### 5\. IV Treatment

After confirming the treatment details, the IV therapy is administered safely by our nurse. Your condition is monitored carefully throughout the session so that the experience remains comfortable, professional, and medically appropriate.

## If You Prefer an In-Clinic Appointment

Patients who prefer an in-clinic appointment may receive treatment in a calm and private clinic setting. While this requires travel to the clinic, some patients prefer the reassurance and atmosphere of an in-person visit.

At Pitonne, **patients who book an in-clinic IV appointment receive JPY 5,000 off the IV menu price**. For some, the convenience of a mobile visit is the priority. For others, the structure of an in-clinic appointment may feel more suitable.

At Pitonne, we believe that the treatment experience should feel not only accessible, but also clear, discreet, and reassuring.

We place particular importance on:

* Making the process easy to understand before booking  
* Conducting appropriate medical review based on the selected treatment and your condition  
* Allowing patients to choose between in-clinic and mobile IV care  
* Providing private and flexible care for patients staying in Tokyo

We often receive inquiries from international travelers, foreign residents in Japan, executives, and busy professionals who value privacy, convenience, and clear medical guidance.

## What to Prepare Before Booking

Before arranging a mobile IV visit or in-clinic appointment, it helps to have the following information ready:

* Your preferred treatment menu  
* Your preferred appointment date and time  
* Your visit address, or whether you prefer to come to the clinic  
* Your medical history  
* Current medications  
* Any allergies

Preparing this information in advance helps us guide your reservation more efficiently and appropriately.

## Cancellation and Changes

Pitonne operates by appointment only. If you need to cancel or change your booking, please contact us at least **24 hours in advance**.

* Up to 24 hours in advance: changes or cancellation accepted  
* Same-day cancellation: 100% of the reserved treatment fee  
* No-show without notice: future bookings may not be accepted

For safety reasons, we may also need to adjust the treatment plan or decline treatment depending on your condition on the day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I receive a mobile IV at my hotel in Tokyo?

If your hotel is within our service area, a mobile IV visit may be possible. Please contact us in advance to confirm details.

### What visit fee is included?

Mobile visit fees are included within 5 km of Nishi Azabu. Beyond that distance, an additional travel fee applies depending on the location.

### Which is better, mobile IV or an in-clinic appointment?

Patients who prefer privacy and reduced travel time often choose mobile IV service. Patients who prefer an in-clinic environment may choose to visit the clinic instead. In-clinic appointments receive a JPY 5,000 discount.

### What payment methods are available?

Payment is generally accepted by credit card and other cashless methods. If you prefer cash payment, please contact us before your appointment.

### When can I cancel?

Cancellations and changes should be made at least 24 hours in advance. Same-day cancellations are charged at 100% of the reserved treatment fee.
