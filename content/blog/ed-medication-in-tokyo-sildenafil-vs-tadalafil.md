When considering ED medication in Tokyo, two commonly prescribed options are sildenafil and tadalafil. Sildenafil is the active ingredient in Viagra, while tadalafil is the active ingredient in Cialis.

Both medications support erectile function by improving blood flow when sexual stimulation occurs. However, they differ in how long they remain effective, how food may affect them, and how they fit into a person’s schedule.

## What Is Erectile Dysfunction?

Erectile dysfunction, or ED, means having ongoing difficulty achieving or maintaining an erection that is firm enough for satisfactory sexual activity.

ED can be related to several factors, including:

- Blood-flow problems
- Diabetes or high blood pressure
- Hormonal conditions
- Certain medications
- Smoking or alcohol use
- Stress, anxiety, or relationship concerns
- Neurological or urological conditions

ED medication can support erectile function, but it does not automatically cause an erection or increase sexual desire. Sexual stimulation is still required.

## Sildenafil vs. Tadalafil: What Is the Difference?

Sildenafil and tadalafil belong to a group of medicines called phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors, or PDE5 inhibitors.

The longer window associated with tadalafil does not mean that an erection continues for 36 hours. It means the medication may support erectile function during that period when sexual stimulation occurs.

According to the Japanese prescribing information, sildenafil is usually taken once before sexual activity, with at least 24 hours between doses. Tadalafil is also generally limited to one dose per day, although the appropriate dose and interval depend on the person’s health and other medications.

## When Might Sildenafil Be Considered?

Sildenafil may be suitable for someone who prefers to take ED medication for a planned occasion rather than having a longer treatment window.

In Japan, sildenafil is generally prescribed at 25 mg or 50 mg and taken approximately one hour before sexual activity. The dose should be determined by a physician based on health history, age, kidney or liver function, other medications, effectiveness, and side effects.

Food may delay the onset of sildenafil. If the medication seems slower to work after a meal, discuss the timing with the prescribing physician rather than taking an additional dose.

## When Might Tadalafil Be Considered?

Tadalafil may be considered when a longer effective window is preferred. Clinical studies have found that tadalafil may support erectile function for up to 36 hours after a dose.

This longer window can reduce the need to plan sexual activity around a narrow period. Tadalafil can also generally be taken without regard to meals.

In Japan, tadalafil is commonly taken approximately one hour before sexual activity. The physician will determine the dose and dosing interval based on effectiveness, tolerability, kidney or liver function, and medication interactions.

## How Is the Right ED Medication Selected?

Neither medication is automatically better for everyone. The choice may depend on:

- How much flexibility you want
- Whether meals are likely to affect the timing
- How often you expect to use the medication
- Previous experience with ED treatment
- Side effects
- Heart, kidney, or liver conditions
- Blood-pressure medication and other prescriptions
- The physician’s assessment

If one medication does not work as expected, do not increase the dose or combine it with another ED medication without medical advice. Timing, food, stimulation, dosage, and the underlying cause of ED can all affect the result.

## Which Medications Require Special Attention?

Before receiving a prescription, provide the physician with a complete list of prescription drugs, over-the-counter medicines, and supplements.

Sildenafil and tadalafil must not be combined with nitrate or nitric oxide–donating medicines because the combination can cause a dangerous drop in blood pressure. Examples include:

- Nitroglycerin
- Isosorbide medications
- Amyl nitrite
- Nicorandil

Riociguat, which is used for certain forms of pulmonary hypertension, is also contraindicated with PDE5 inhibitors.

Additional review may be required if you take alpha-blockers, blood-pressure medication, antifungal medication, antiviral medication, or other drugs that affect how sildenafil or tadalafil is metabolized.

Never stop a prescribed heart or blood-pressure medication to take an ED treatment.

## Possible Side Effects

Possible side effects of ED medication include:

- Headache
- Facial flushing
- Nasal congestion
- Indigestion
- Dizziness
- Palpitations

Sildenafil may cause temporary changes in vision, including altered color perception. Tadalafil may be associated with back pain or muscle discomfort in some people.

Seek urgent medical care if you experience:

- An erection lasting four hours or longer
- Chest pain
- Fainting or severe dizziness
- Sudden loss or significant reduction of vision
- Sudden hearing loss
- Difficulty breathing or another severe reaction

If chest pain occurs after taking ED medication, tell emergency personnel exactly what medication you took and when you took it. Do not use nitrate medication unless instructed by an emergency physician who knows about the ED medication.

## ED Can Be a Reason to Review Your General Health

ED is not always an isolated sexual-health issue. It may be associated with diabetes, high blood pressure, abnormal cholesterol levels, cardiovascular disease, obesity, smoking, hormonal conditions, or medication effects.

The [European Association of Urology](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/sexual-and-reproductive-health/chapter/management-of-erectile-dysfunction) recommends evaluating possible underlying conditions and modifiable risk factors when assessing ED.

If symptoms are new, persistent, or becoming more frequent, discuss them honestly during the consultation. Additional evaluation may be appropriate instead of repeatedly using medication without reviewing the possible cause.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is sildenafil or tadalafil stronger?

One is not universally stronger than the other. Sildenafil has a shorter treatment window, while tadalafil remains active longer. The better option depends on the desired timing, medical history, other medications, side effects, and individual response.

### Can I take sildenafil and tadalafil together?

Do not combine sildenafil, tadalafil, or other ED medications unless a physician has specifically instructed you to do so. Combining them may increase the risk of low blood pressure and other adverse effects.

### Does ED medication increase sexual desire?

No. Sildenafil and tadalafil support the physical process involved in an erection when sexual stimulation occurs. They are not aphrodisiacs and do not directly increase libido.
