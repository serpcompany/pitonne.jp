A Skin Brightening & Sun Care IV Drip is a wellness treatment that contains glutathione and may include other skin-supporting ingredients. In Japan, it is also commonly called a “Shiratama IV” or glutathione IV.

At Pitonne, this treatment is offered to support skin wellness, antioxidant balance, and recovery during periods of sun exposure. A physician reviews your health history, current condition, and goals before recommending the IV formulation.

## What Is Glutathione?

Glutathione is a substance naturally produced in the body. It is made from three amino acids: glutamate, cysteine, and glycine.

It plays a role in the body’s antioxidant and redox systems. In aesthetic medicine, glutathione is used to support skin condition and a brighter, healthier-looking appearance.

## What Does a Skin Brightening & Sun Care IV Drip Contain?

Glutathione is the main ingredient. Some clinics combine it with vitamin C or other supportive nutrients.

However, treatments offered under similar names do not always contain the same ingredients or doses. Before treatment, ask about:

- The amount of glutathione
- Other ingredients included in the IV
- The total fluid volume
- Treatment duration
- The recommended frequency
- Whether a physician’s consultation is required

## What Does Research Say About IV Glutathione for Skin Brightening?

Glutathione is widely used in aesthetic settings, but only a limited number of studies have specifically examined intravenous glutathione for changes in skin tone.

A 2016 placebo-controlled study compared glutathione with vitamin C against normal saline. Participants received treatment twice a week for six weeks.

Among the 32 participants who completed the study, improvement was reported in 6 of 16 participants receiving the glutathione combination and 3 of 16 participants receiving the placebo.

The study was small, the formulation contained vitamin C and other ingredients, and most of the reported change gradually decreased after treatment ended. Therefore, it cannot establish the independent effect of glutathione, an ideal treatment schedule, or how long any change may last. [Efficacy of intravenous glutathione vs. placebo for skin tone lightening](https://doi.org/10.66344/jpad.v26i3.18)

One treatment should not be expected to produce a dramatic change, and individual responses may differ.

## What Should You Tell the Nurse or Physician Before Treatment?

Before receiving a glutathione IV, provide information about:

- Your current symptoms and general health
- Allergies
- Previous reactions to injections or IV therapy
- Liver, kidney, or other medical conditions
- Prescription and over-the-counter medication
- Vitamins and supplements
- Pregnancy or breastfeeding
- Recent aesthetic procedures or medical treatments
- Your treatment goals and preferred timing

If you have recently received another wellness or aesthetic IV, provide its name, ingredients, and treatment date. This helps identify ingredients that may overlap.

## Why Does the Quality of the Injectable Product Matter?

It is important to consider more than the ingredient name. Glutathione used intravenously should be appropriate for injection and handled, stored, and prepared under suitable medical conditions.

In 2026, the US Food and Drug Administration and Centers for Disease Control and Prevention reported illnesses associated with injectable glutathione compounded using dietary supplement-grade ingredients. Those ingredients were not appropriate for sterile injectable products and were linked to endotoxin contamination.

These reports involved specific compounded products outside Japan. They do not mean that every glutathione injection is affected, but they demonstrate why ingredient sourcing, sterile preparation, and product quality matter. [FDA safety information on injectable glutathione](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/fda-reminds-compounders-not-use-dietary-supplement-grade-glutathione-injectables)

If you have concerns, ask how the product is managed and whom to contact if symptoms develop after treatment.

## How Often Is Glutathione IV Therapy Given?

There is no single evidence-based treatment schedule that is appropriate for everyone.

Frequency depends on the formulation, treatment goal, health condition, and response after each appointment. Receiving treatment more frequently does not guarantee a greater or faster change.

A Skin Brightening & Sun Care IV Drip is also not a replacement for sunscreen, moisturization, sleep, nutrition, or other daily skin care. It should be considered alongside regular sun protection and healthy lifestyle habits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Shiratama IV the same as a glutathione IV?

In Japan, “Shiratama IV” commonly refers to an aesthetic IV containing glutathione. However, the dose and additional ingredients vary between clinics, so the complete formulation should be confirmed before treatment.

### Is injectable glutathione the same as a glutathione supplement?

No. Although the ingredient name may be the same, the route of administration and product requirements are different. A dietary supplement product must not be used as an injectable product.

### Can I receive only one treatment?

A single appointment may be possible, but the response varies between individuals. Discuss your goals, timing, and expectations with the physician before deciding on a treatment schedule.

### Can glutathione be combined with other ingredients?

Other ingredients may be included depending on your health condition and goals. Do not add ingredients based only on their name. Tell the physician about your medication and supplements before the formulation is selected.
