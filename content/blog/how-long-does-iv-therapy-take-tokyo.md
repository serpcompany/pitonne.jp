If you are considering IV therapy in Tokyo for the first time, one of the most practical questions is simple: how long does IV therapy take?

The answer depends on the treatment selected, the consultation process, your current condition, and whether you choose an in-clinic or mobile appointment. As a general guide, the IV administration itself at Pitonne usually takes approximately 30 to 60 minutes. For first-time patients, it is recommended to allow about one hour in total, including the consultation and treatment explanation.

This guide explains what to expect before, during, and after an IV therapy appointment at Pitonne.

## Quick Answer: How Long Does IV Therapy Take?

At Pitonne, the IV administration itself generally takes about 30 to 60 minutes. A first appointment may take approximately one hour in total, including the consultation and explanation of the treatment.

Mobile appointments may require additional planning because the nurse travels to your home, hotel, or office. The exact timing can vary depending on the selected treatment, your condition on the day, and whether an additional physician consultation is required. The duration should always be confirmed when booking.

## What Determines the Duration of an IV Therapy Appointment?

### The Treatment Selected

Different IV menus may contain different formulations and volumes. Some treatments may be relatively brief, while others may require more time for administration and observation.

The name of a treatment alone does not determine how long the appointment will take. Confirm the specific contents, expected duration, and relevant precautions before treatment.

### The Initial Consultation

For first-time patients, the appointment may include a review of your current condition, medical history, medications, allergies, and treatment goals.

Depending on the treatment selected and your individual situation, an online consultation with a partner physician may also be required before the IV can be administered.

### Your Condition on the Day

Your condition may be different on the day of your appointment compared with the day you booked. Treatment is therefore not automatically guaranteed.

The medical team may decide that treatment should be postponed or that another type of medical evaluation is more appropriate. Pitonne prioritizes medical screening and individualized decision-making before proceeding.

### In-Clinic or Mobile Appointment

Pitonne offers both in-clinic appointments and mobile IV visits within the service area. Mobile visits may be arranged at a home, hotel, or office, depending on the location and appointment availability.

When booking mobile IV therapy in Tokyo, ask how the visit timing will be arranged and whether additional travel considerations apply.

## What Happens During an IV Therapy Appointment?

1.  Booking and advance information: You may be asked for your name, contact details, preferred date and time, location, medical history, medications, allergies, and the treatment you are considering.

2.  Consultation and medical review: The team reviews your current condition and the reason you are considering IV therapy.

3.  Treatment explanation: Before the IV begins, the treatment contents, expected duration, fees, possible side effects, and aftercare guidance should be explained clearly.

4.  IV administration: The IV itself usually takes approximately 30 to 60 minutes at Pitonne, depending on the selected treatment and individual circumstances.

5.  Aftercare guidance: After the IV is complete, the team explains relevant precautions and what to watch for after treatment.

## How Much Time Should You Allow for a Mobile IV Visit?

If you are booking mobile IV therapy in Tokyo, it is best to keep your schedule flexible. The IV administration may take approximately 30 to 60 minutes, but the overall visit may also include arrival and preparation, medical review, treatment explanation, IV placement, infusion time, and aftercare guidance.

For this reason, avoid scheduling an important meeting or travel connection immediately after your appointment. If you are staying at a hotel, inform the hotel team if access or privacy arrangements are needed.

## Is IV Therapy Always Appropriate?

No. IV therapy is not automatically appropriate for every person or every situation. Suitability depends on factors such as your current symptoms, medical history, medications, allergies, and the contents of the proposed IV.

IV therapy should not be used as a substitute for emergency medical care. If you have difficulty breathing, chest pain, severe dizziness, confusion, or an inability to keep fluids down, seek urgent medical attention. General IV treatment guidance also emphasizes monitoring for swelling, pain, redness, bleeding, or signs of infection around the IV site.

Tell the clinic about your current condition, medical history, medications, allergies, and any recent treatments. This information helps the medical team assess whether the selected service may be appropriate.
