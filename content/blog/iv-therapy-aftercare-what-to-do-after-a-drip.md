# IV Therapy Aftercare: What to Do After a Drip

If you are considering IV therapy in Tokyo, you may wonder what to do after the infusion is complete. IV therapy aftercare depends on the treatment contents, the amount administered, your medical history, and how you feel on the day.

In general, follow the instructions provided by the medical team, observe the IV insertion site, and allow enough time before demanding activities. If you develop severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, seek medical attention rather than waiting for a routine follow-up.

## What Should You Do After IV Therapy?

Before leaving, confirm:

- What treatment and ingredients were administered
- What symptoms you should monitor
- Whether you should change your usual activities
- How to contact the clinic if you have concerns
- Whether you should avoid exercise, alcohol, or hot baths

There is no single aftercare routine for every IV drip. Guidance may differ between vitamin IV therapy, medication infusions, and other treatments.

You do not necessarily need to stay in bed after IV therapy. However, if you are receiving treatment for the first time, consider avoiding an important meeting, long journey, or strenuous exercise immediately afterward. This gives you time to notice how you feel.

## How Should You Monitor the IV Site?

Mild tenderness or bruising may occur where the needle was inserted. Continue to observe the area after treatment.

Contact the clinic or a healthcare professional if you notice:

- Increasing pain
- Worsening redness or swelling
- Continued bleeding
- Increasing warmth
- Drainage or pus
- Fever of 38°C or higher

MedlinePlus advises contacting a healthcare provider for redness, swelling, bruising, pain, bleeding, or a fever of 38°C or higher after IV treatment. [MedlinePlus: IV treatment at home](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000496.htm)

If bleeding continues, apply pressure as instructed by the medical team and contact the provider.

## Can You Exercise, Drink Alcohol, or Take a Bath?

There is no universal rule for all IV treatments. The appropriate timing may depend on the infusion contents, your medications, and your health condition.

Ask the medical team before exercising intensely, drinking alcohol, using a sauna, or taking a hot bath. If you feel dizzy, nauseated, weak, or generally unwell, postpone strenuous activities and contact the clinic for advice.

You should also follow the medical team’s instructions about food and fluids. Do not force yourself to drink excessive amounts of water unless a qualified healthcare professional has recommended it.

## When Should You Seek Urgent Medical Help?

Seek urgent medical attention if you experience:

- Difficulty breathing
- Chest pain
- Fainting
- Severe dizziness
- Swelling of the face, lips, tongue, or throat
- A very fast heartbeat
- Confusion or a sudden change in consciousness

These symptoms should not be assumed to be a normal response to IV therapy. MedlinePlus also advises seeking emergency help for breathing problems, a fast heart rate, dizziness, or chest pain after IV treatment. [MedlinePlus: IV treatment at home](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000496.htm)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long should I rest after IV therapy?

There is no fixed rest period for everyone. Some people may return to their normal routine, while others may prefer a quieter schedule. Ask the medical team based on your treatment and condition.

### Is bruising after an IV normal?

Mild bruising or tenderness may occur after needle insertion. Increasing pain, swelling, redness, warmth, bleeding, drainage, or fever should be reported to a healthcare professional.

### Can I drink alcohol after IV therapy?

The answer depends on the treatment, your medications, and your health condition. Ask the medical team whether alcohol should be avoided and when it may be appropriate.

## Conclusion

IV therapy aftercare depends on the treatment contents, the amount administered, and your individual condition.

After a drip, follow the instructions provided by the medical team and monitor the IV site for changes. Contact the clinic if symptoms worsen, and seek urgent medical attention for breathing difficulty, chest pain, fainting, or severe dizziness.
